# Change My Mind

White field. The product appears as the dark screens from the app. Voice starts at 0:10.

## Opening (0:00–0:10)

We yell. We hate. We assume we're better.

## The problem (0:10–0:25)

We have a problem. We've lost the most important part of a functional society: civil debate. On both sides, we yell, we hate, and we assume we're better than everyone else. Think you're different? Try to change my mind.

## The ask (0:25–0:30)

We don't want people to yell. We want them to share ideas and actually listen. Two marks pass an idea, then a line draws between them.

## The day (0:30–0:56)

Every day, there's one new question tied to what's happening in the world, from politics to pop culture. On that line the Capitol sits large in the corner, then the Odyssey poster pops in. Today's question through the end is one black panel. A different color presses in from each edge. The Taylor Swift question loads in, Huey takes the other side, the three-minute clock starts, then the analytics stay up.

## The judge (0:56–1:09)

Then an AI judge scores respect and argument quality, not which side is right. Disrespect loses. You see where you landed, get a few takeaways, and your streak updates. Come back tomorrow for a new topic.

## Huey (1:09–1:19)

So who is Huey? Huey is a collection of opinions. He takes the winning ideas, fact-checks them, and learns from them. Huey is everyone who has argued his side.

## The build (1:19–1:37)

We built it in Cursor. Firebase handles sign-in, the daily lobby, and streaks. Two Grok agents run in parallel, one speaking and one judging, and every change is tested on sample debates before it ships. Understanding, not conflict is its own card. One discussion at a time.

## The demo (1:37–1:51)

The dark product, three screens: today, voice or text, then the result.
