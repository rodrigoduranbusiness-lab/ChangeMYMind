# Change My Mind

White field. The product appears as the dark screens from the app. Voice starts at 0:10.

## Opening (0:00–0:10)

We yell. We hate. We assume we're better.

## The problem (0:10–0:25)

We have a problem. We've lost the most important part of a functional society: civil debate. On both sides, we yell, we hate, and we assume we're better than everyone else. Think you're different? Try to change my mind.

## The ask (0:25–0:30)

We don't want people to yell. We want them to share ideas and actually listen.

## The day (0:30–0:56)

Every day, there's one new question tied to what's happening in the world, from politics to pop culture. Today's: is Taylor Swift's impact on music positive or negative? Tap a side. Huey takes the other. Argue out loud or type, and a three-minute clock starts. In voice, Huey argues back live. In text, you're matched with another person and Huey moderates, or you debate Huey if no one's waiting.

## The judge (0:56–1:09)

Then an AI judge scores respect and argument quality, not which side is right. Disrespect loses. You see where you landed, get a few takeaways, and your streak updates. Come back tomorrow for a new topic.

## Huey (1:09–1:19)

So who is Huey? Huey is a collection of opinions. He takes the winning ideas, fact-checks them, and learns from them. Huey is everyone who has argued his side.

## The build (1:19–1:37)

We built it in Cursor. Firebase handles sign-in, the daily lobby, and streaks. Two Grok agents run in parallel, one speaking and one judging, and every change is tested on sample debates before it ships. Debate should be rooted in understanding, not conflict. One discussion at a time.

## The demo (1:37–1:51)

The dark product, three screens: today, voice or text, then the result.
