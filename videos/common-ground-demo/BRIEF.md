## Intent

A bare-bones 2-minute motion graphic for Common Ground. It is a daily game, like Wordle: one topic a day (for example, "Is Taylor Swift good for music"), three minutes to argue the other side out loud or in text, one winner and one loser. A disrespectful argument loses. Winning is a good take, not pushing the other person down. If you win, your voice and your takes start to shape how that side talks. The mission is more civil discourse. People come back for the streak, and because they want to win.

Huey is a cluster of shapes made of the people who spoke on that day's topic. Arguing out loud means arguing fact-checked takes from the other side that day. You are arguing with Huey, not with an empty model.

The first pass is deliberately plain: black field, white Computer Modern, film grain, hard cuts, short lines typed onto the screen. No music, no voiceover, no fancy motion yet.
